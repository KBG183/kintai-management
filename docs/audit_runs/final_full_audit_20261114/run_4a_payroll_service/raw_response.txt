| F-01 | Japanese Labor Standards Act Art 37: Night Work Premium Calculation | P0 | FAIL | `PayrollService.java` (Line ~180-190): `PayrollCalculator.calculateDailyPay` is called with `nightWorkMinutes` and `overtimeMinutes` separately. The service sums `totalNightAllowance` and `totalOvertimeAllowance` independently. | **Critical Logic Flaw**: The code calculates Night Allowance and Overtime Allowance as separate additive components. However, under JLSA Art 37, if work occurs during night hours (22:00-05:00) AND is also overtime (>8h/day), the premium must be **combined** (Base + 25% Night + 25% Overtime = 150% of base, or effectively +50% extra). The current logic likely adds `Base * 0.25` for night and `Base * 0.25` for overtime separately, which is mathematically correct for the *sum*, BUT the statutory rounding logic (`applyStatutoryMonthlyRounding`) treats them as independent buckets. More critically, the `PayrollCalculator` implementation is not visible, but the service layer does not explicitly handle the "Night + Overtime" overlap deduction from the night bucket before applying the 25% night premium, which is a common source of error. If `nightWorkMinutes` includes the overtime portion, and `overtimeMinutes` also includes it, the base pay might be double-counted or the premium logic might be misapplied depending on the hidden `PayrollCalculator` logic. Given the strict requirement for "Night + Overtime concurrent overlap: +50% combined extra pay", the separation of concerns in the DTOs (`nightWorkMinutes` vs `overtimeMinutes`) without a clear "overlap" field suggests a high risk of miscalculation. |
